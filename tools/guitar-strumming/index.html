    Format and supported chords

    Use D for a downstroke, U for an upstroke, and - for no strum.

    Use Chord@N for a chord change at slot N. The change occurs before a strum at the same slot.

    Use xN after one bar to repeat that bar N times in total.

    Supported chords:

    A Am Bb Bm C Cm D Dm Em F F#m G Am7 C7 Cmaj7 D7 G7 Cadd9 Dsus4 G5 Am/G C/E D/F# G/B
