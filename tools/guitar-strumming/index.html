Audio prototype

Guitar strumming audio test

This page tests synthesized guitar sound and accurate browser scheduling. It is not the complete teaching tool.

Demonstration pattern

The loop uses 4/4 time, an eight-slot grid, and 100 beats per minute.

chords:
| C G/B@8 | G/B | Am F@8 | F |

strum:
| D - D U - U D U | x4

The changes at slot 8 occur before the upstroke at slot 8.

What to check

  1. Compare the C downstroke and upstroke.
  2. Play the loop and listen for a steady rhythm.
  3. Listen for the chord changes at the final slot of bars 1 and 3.
  4. Simulate a stall and confirm that you do not hear a burst of late strums.
  5. Stop playback and confirm that the sound ends.
